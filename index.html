Everything a shared home needs, nothing it doesn't

Made for couples and roommates who split rent, groceries and the rest, and just want the numbers to be right.

Split it your way

Divide an expense evenly, by exact amounts or by percentage. Tabode checks the math so the shares always add up.

Mark each share paid

Paid someone back for one bill? Mark just that share as paid, on any device, and undo it if you tapped the wrong one.

Settle up at once

See everything still open in both directions, net it out to one amount, and record how you paid when you square up.

Organized by category

Rent, groceries, utilities, dining, pets and more, each with its own icon, plus the date paid and who added it.
